Frequently asked questionsAbout home interiors

Everything you need to know before you start your home with INLAY.

01What do home interiors cost in Hyderabad?

It depends on carpet area, scope and finish — a 2BHK kitchen and wardrobes sits far below a full 3BHK. Share your floor plan and we'll give you an itemised number for your home, not a range.

02How long does INLAY take to complete my home?

30–45 days from order confirmation to handover. The date goes in writing, and if we miss it, we pay your rent for the month.

03Do you only do modular kitchens, or single rooms too?

Both. Turnkey homes, modular kitchens, wardrobes, furniture, home appliances, and decor and styling — one room or the whole house.

04What makes INLAY different?

One team from design to service. An itemised quote that doesn't move. A handover date in writing, backed by the rent promise. And a ten-year warranty on what we build.

05Is every design customised?

Yes. Every unit is made to your measurements and your floor plan. Nothing is picked off a catalogue shelf and forced to fit.

06Can I see your work before I decide?

Yes — visit the experience centre. Open the drawers, close them, check the finishes. You'll also see your own home in 3D before you sign anything.

07Do you give a warranty?

Ten years, covering factory-made units and the hardware inside them. After that, our care team is still a call away.

08Is the design consultation really free?

Yes. Consultation, 3D design and quote cost nothing and carry no obligation.

09How do I book a consultation?

Fill in the estimate form, call +91 86888 87979 or message us on WhatsApp. A consultant calls you back the same day.

10Do you work on apartments and villas?

Apartments, villas and independent houses, anywhere in Hyderabad. New possession or a home you're already living in.
